Tracing the Cosmic Ecosystem.

Exploring how galaxies exchange matter, metals, and energy with their surroundings across space and time

ABOUT

I am Rieko Momose, an observational astronomer studying how galaxies form and evolve over cosmic time.
Across an enormous range of physical scales, from cosmological structures to individual stars and black holes, galaxies continuously exchange and recycle matter, energy, and radiation, making them a kind of ecosystem, much like a biological one. Our research explores this cosmic ecosystem by addressing how galaxies exchange matter, metals, and energy with their surroundings, using multiwavelength observations of the intergalactic, circumgalactic, and interstellar medium.
