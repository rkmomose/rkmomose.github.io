Public Lectures

I enjoy sharing the excitement of astronomy with people of all ages and backgrounds. I have given numerous public lectures and participated in astronomy outreach programs across Japan, including events at the University of Tokyo, Katsushika City Museum, Minato Science Museum, Science Agora, and Tsukuba Expo Center. I also organized a series of STEM outreach event for young children at a University of Tokyo nursery school in Kashiwa campus. During my time in the United States, I participated in STEM and career fairs at local schools and public outreach events at Carnegie Observatories.
